# Code of Conduct

This repo is open to contributions from people who act with basic respect and
good faith.

## Expectations

- Be respectful.
- Stay on topic.
- Do not harass, threaten, or insult other people.
- Do not spam issues, pull requests, or comments.
- Do not impersonate another person or misrepresent your intent.

## Maintenance

Maintainers may remove or close comments, issues, or pull requests that are
abusive, disruptive, or off topic.

## Scope

This applies to contributions, issue threads, pull requests, and other project
spaces tied to this repo.

## Reporting

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers through the
repo or use the security contact only for security problems.
