# CAD

CAD uses computer tools to create precise digital models of parts, assemblies, and drawings.

## Core ideas

- Parametric models use dimensions and constraints to control geometry.
- Assemblies combine parts so designers can check fit and motion.
- Drawings communicate dimensions, tolerances, and manufacturing details.

## Common methods and terms

- Sketches are usually the starting point for a model.
- Constraints and dimensions make the design editable and repeatable.
- Version control and revision history matter when designs change.

## Typical questions

- Is the geometry fully defined?
- Will the part assemble and move as intended?
- Are the tolerances realistic for manufacturing?
