# Project Management

Project management coordinates people, tasks, time, and cost so a project can meet its scope and finish successfully.

## Core ideas

- Scope, schedule, cost, quality, and risk must be balanced.
- Stakeholders and communication plans matter as much as task lists.
- A project plan should make responsibilities and milestones visible.

## Common methods and terms

- Work breakdown structures divide the project into manageable tasks.
- Milestones, dependencies, and the critical path help track time.
- Regular status reviews catch delays and risks before they grow.

## Typical questions

- What is the project actually trying to deliver?
- Which tasks must happen before others can start?
- What risk could most easily derail the schedule?
