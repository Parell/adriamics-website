# Quality Control

Quality control keeps a process within acceptable limits by measuring variation and acting on defects early.

## Core ideas

- Variation is inevitable, so the question is whether it stays within limits.
- Control charts and sampling help separate common variation from special causes.
- Capability describes whether a process can meet the required specification.

## Common methods and terms

- Inspection, sampling plans, and process audits are common tools.
- Root-cause analysis helps find why a defect happened.
- Corrective action should reduce repeat problems, not just fix one part.

## Typical questions

- Is the process stable?
- Does the output meet the specification consistently?
- What change will lower defects the most?
