# Signals and Systems

Signals and systems studies how input signals are transformed by linear systems and how those transformations can be analyzed in time and frequency.

## Core ideas

- A signal carries information, and a system maps an input signal to an output signal.
- Linear time-invariant systems are especially useful because they can be analyzed with convolution and frequency response.
- Time-domain and frequency-domain views describe the same system from different angles.

## Common methods and terms

- Convolution gives the output of an LTI system from its impulse response.
- Fourier and Laplace tools help analyze spectra and stability.
- Sampling connects continuous-time signals to discrete-time processing.

## Typical questions

- Is the system linear and time-invariant?
- What does the impulse response say about the output?
- How does the signal change after sampling or filtering?
