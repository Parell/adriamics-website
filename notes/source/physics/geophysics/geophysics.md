# Geophysics

Geophysics applies physics to the Earth, from its interior structure to its gravity, magnetism, and surface processes.

## Core ideas

- The Earth is studied as a layered system with a crust, mantle, outer core, and inner core.
- Seismic waves reveal information about interior structure and material properties.
- Gravity, magnetism, and heat flow help explain geologic and tectonic processes.

## Common methods and terms

- Seismology is a major tool for probing the interior.
- Remote sensing and geodesy help measure the planet at large scale.
- Models connect physical properties to plate motion, earthquakes, and resource distribution.

## Typical questions

- Which Earth layer or process is being described?
- What measurement gives the evidence?
- How does the physics explain the geologic pattern?
