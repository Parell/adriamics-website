# Plasma Physics

Plasma physics studies ionized gases whose charged particles interact collectively through electric and magnetic fields.

## Core ideas

- A plasma contains free charges, so it responds strongly to fields.
- Debye shielding and collective behavior distinguish plasma from an ordinary gas.
- Magnetized plasmas are important in space physics, fusion, and astrophysical systems.

## Common methods and terms

- Magnetohydrodynamics treats plasma on a fluid-like scale.
- Wave motion, confinement, and instability analysis are central topics.
- Diagnostics measure temperature, density, and field structure.

## Typical questions

- Is the plasma weakly or strongly magnetized?
- What collective effect is being described?
- How do the fields shape the motion of the charges?
