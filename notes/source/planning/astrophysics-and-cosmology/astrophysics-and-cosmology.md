# Astrophysics and Cosmology

Astrophysics and cosmology study stars, galaxies, and the large-scale structure and evolution of the universe.

## Core ideas

- Astrophysics looks at the physical processes inside and around astronomical objects.
- Cosmology studies the universe as a whole, including its expansion and age.
- Redshift, spectra, and brightness measurements reveal distance and motion.

## Common methods and terms

- The H-R diagram, standard candles, and spectral lines are key tools.
- Dark matter and dark energy are part of the modern cosmology picture.
- Observations across many wavelengths help build a complete picture of a source.

## Typical questions

- Is the question about an object or the whole universe?
- What observation gives the evidence?
- Which physical process explains the behavior?
