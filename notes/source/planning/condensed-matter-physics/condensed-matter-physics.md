# Condensed Matter Physics

Condensed matter physics studies the collective behavior of many-particle systems such as solids, liquids, and structured materials.

## Core ideas

- Crystal structure and bonding shape the behavior of a material.
- Band structure explains why some materials are metals, insulators, or semiconductors.
- Collective effects include magnetism, superconductivity, and phase transitions.

## Common methods and terms

- Diffraction and spectroscopy reveal structure and excitations.
- Transport measurements show how charges, heat, and spin move through a material.
- Models often focus on simplified lattices or effective particles to capture the key physics.

## Typical questions

- What arrangement of atoms or electrons is being studied?
- Which property changes when the temperature or field changes?
- Is the behavior explained by single particles or collective motion?
