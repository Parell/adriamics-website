# Instrumentation

Instrumentation focuses on measuring physical quantities accurately and turning them into usable signals.

## Core ideas

- A sensor converts a physical quantity into an electrical or digital signal.
- Calibration connects the instrument reading to a known standard.
- Noise, drift, and resolution limit how precise a measurement can be.

## Common methods and terms

- Signal conditioning may use amplification, filtering, shielding, or isolation.
- Analog-to-digital conversion makes measurements available to software.
- Uncertainty analysis is part of the measurement process, not an afterthought.

## Typical questions

- What quantity is actually being measured?
- Which error source is largest?
- How do calibration and filtering improve the reading?
