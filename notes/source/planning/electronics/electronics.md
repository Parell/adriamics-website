# Electronics

Electronics studies circuits that control current, voltage, and signal behavior using passive and active components.

## Core ideas

- Voltage, current, resistance, and power are the base quantities for circuit analysis.
- Diodes, transistors, and op-amps are the main building blocks of many circuits.
- Biasing and loading determine whether a circuit behaves as intended.

## Common methods and terms

- Circuit analysis often combines Ohm's law with Kirchhoff's laws.
- Amplifiers, filters, rectifiers, and switches are common applications.
- Small-signal and operating-point ideas help separate DC and AC behavior.

## Typical questions

- Is the component operating in the correct region?
- How much power is dissipated?
- Does the load change the circuit behavior?
