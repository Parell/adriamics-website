# Particle and Nuclear Physics

Particle and nuclear physics study the fundamental constituents of matter, their interactions, and the structure of nuclei.

## Core ideas

- The Standard Model organizes the known fundamental particles and forces.
- Nuclear physics focuses on protons, neutrons, binding energy, and radioactive decay.
- Conservation laws help determine what reactions are allowed.

## Common methods and terms

- Scattering experiments reveal particle properties.
- Detectors measure tracks, energy, and decay products.
- Reaction diagrams and decay chains help track what happens in a process.

## Typical questions

- Which particles or nuclei are involved?
- What conservation law constrains the reaction?
- What measurement would identify the process?
