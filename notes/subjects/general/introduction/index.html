Standalone, crawlable note page

UES, the Universal Education System, is a growing library of subject notes organized to make technical topics easier to study, review, and revisit.

This section is meant to be a practical study workspace. Each subject is split into focused pages so you can move directly to the topic you need instead of digging through a single long document.

Best way to study here

Start with the topic list for the subject you need, open the page that matches your current question, and use the headings to work from the basic ideas toward the more detailed material, Put pen to paper and work from topic to topic, you got this.

How to use these notes

  • Choose a subject from the tabs at the top of the page.

  • Use the guide panel on the left to open a specific topic within that subject.

  • Use the table of contents on the right to jump to sections inside the current page.

  • Move between pages as needed when reviewing a class, preparing for an exam, or filling a gap in your understanding.

What to expect

  • Notes are written as concise overviews, not full textbooks.

  • Topics are grouped by subject so related material stays together.

  • Some sections may still be placeholders while the library is expanding.
