What core idea is introduced in Entry notation?

Solution

One short answer is: If $A$ is a matrix, its entry in row $i$ and column $j$ is written as: $$ a {ij} $$ For example: $$ A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \end{bmatrix} $$ then: $$ a {12} = 2,\quad a {21} = 4 $$ ---

1.14Review: Matrix multiplication

2.11Review: Determinant properties

Solution

2.14Review: Inverse matrices

9. Inverse matrices

What is the main idea of Inverse matrices?

Solution

One short answer is: If $A$ is square, its inverse $A^{-1}$ satisfies: $$ AA^{-1} = A^{-1}A = I $$ An inverse exists exactly when $A$ is nonsingular.

2.15Review: Why inverses matter

What is the main idea of Why inverses matter?

Solution

One short answer is: If $Ax=b$ and $A^{-1}$ exists, then: $$ x = A^{-1}b $$ This solves the system in one step, though computing the inverse directly is often not the best numerical method.

2.16Review: Inverse of a 2 by 2 matrix

Inverse of a 2 by 2 matrix

What is the main idea of Inverse of a 2 by 2 matrix?

Solution

One short answer is: For: $$ A = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} $$ if $ad-bc \ne 0$, then: $$ A^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad-bc} \begin{bmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{bmatrix} $$

2.17Review: Inverse properties

3.4Review: Rank

Rank

What is the main idea of Rank?

Solution

One short answer is: The rank of a matrix is the number of pivot columns, equivalently the dimension of the column space. It is also the dimension of the row space.

3.5Review: Nullity

Nullity

What is the main idea of Nullity?

Solution

One short answer is: The nullity of a matrix is the dimension of its null space: $$ \mathcal{N}(A) = \{x : Ax = 0\} $$

3.6Review: Rank-nullity theorem

Rank-nullity theorem

What is the main idea of Rank-nullity theorem?

Solution

One short answer is: For an $m \times n$ matrix: $$ \operatorname{rank}(A) + \operatorname{nullity}(A) = n $$ This is one of the most useful bookkeeping identities in linear algebra.

3.7Review: Column space

Column space

What is the main idea of Column space?

Solution

One short answer is: The column space is the span of the columns of $A$. It is the set of all vectors $b$ for which $Ax=b$ has at least one solution.

3.8Review: Row space

Row space

What is the main idea of Row space?

Solution

One short answer is: The row space is the span of the rows of $A$. Row operations preserve the row space up to equivalence of the row span.

3.9Review: Null space

Null space

What is the main idea of Null space?

Solution

One short answer is: The null space contains all vectors mapped to zero by $A$. If the null space contains only the zero vector, then $A$ has full column rank. ---
