1.1Derive a Lagrange equation

Exam I | Problem 1.1 | Euler–Lagrange equations · Lagrangian mechanics

For $L=\tfrac12m\dot x^2-V(x)$, derive the equation of motion.

-

Solution

$$ \frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot x}=m\dot x, \qquad \frac{\partial L}{\partial x}=-V'(x), $$

so Euler–Lagrange gives $m\ddot x=-V'(x)$.

1.2Identify a cyclic coordinate

Exam I | Problem 1.2 | Cyclic coordinates · Conservation laws

If $L$ does not contain $\theta$, what quantity is conserved?

-

Solution

$p_\theta=\partial L/\partial\dot\theta$ is conserved because $d p_\theta/dt=\partial L/\partial\theta=0$.

1.3Use canonical equations

Exam I | Problem 1.3 | Hamiltonian mechanics · Canonical equations

For $H=p^2/(2m)+kx^2/2$, find $\dot x$ and $\dot p$.

-

Solution

$$ \dot x=\frac{\partial H}{\partial p}=\frac{p}{m}, \qquad \dot p=-\frac{\partial H}{\partial x}=-kx. $$

1.4Compute a Poisson bracket

Exam I | Problem 1.4 | Poisson brackets · Time evolution

Compute $\{x,p^2\}$.

-

Solution

$$ \{x,p^2\}=\frac{\partial x}{\partial x}\frac{\partial p^2}{\partial p}=2p. $$

1.5Separate a Hamilton–Jacobi equation

Exam I | Problem 1.5 | Hamilton–Jacobi theory · Separation of variables

For a free particle with $H=p^2/(2m)$, find a separated principal function with constant momentum $p_0$.

-

Solution

Set $S=W(x)-Et$. The Hamilton–Jacobi equation gives $(W')^2/(2m)=E$. Choosing $W'=p_0$ gives $E=p_0^2/(2m)$ and

$$ S(x,t)=p_0x-\frac{p_0^2}{2m}t+C. $$
