2.7Review: Commutators

What is the main idea of Commutators?

Solution

What is the main idea of Uncertainty and superposition?

Solution

One short answer is: The Heisenberg uncertainty principle states $$ \Delta x\,\Delta p \ge \frac{\hbar}{2} $$ More generally, non-commuting observables cannot both be sharply defined in the same state.

2.10Review: Superposition

What is the main idea of Superposition?

Solution

2.11Review: Relative phase
