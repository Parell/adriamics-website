What this site is for

2026-03-31 - 3 min read

This page is a temporary placeholder, but the intent is straightforward: keep the homepage focused on selected work, then use the blog for short notes that are useful to future me and anyone reading along.

I want the writing here to be concrete. That means fewer generic posts, more specific notes about software, physics, engineering judgment, and the tradeoffs that show up when those things meet actual constraints.

The archive link on the homepage will always point at the newest post first, so the front page stays current without manual editing. That is the piece I wanted in place before I write any real content.
