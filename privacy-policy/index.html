Privacy Policy

Last updated May 3, 2026.

This policy describes how the Adriamics website handles information submitted through contact and careers forms.

Information we collect

When you contact us or apply for a role, we may collect your name, email address, role interests, resume or portfolio link, and the message you choose to send.

How we use it

We use that information to review inquiries, respond to submissions, and evaluate talent network interest.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We only share it when needed to operate the site, evaluate a submission, or comply with law.

Retention

We keep submissions only as long as needed for review, follow-up, and recordkeeping related to the request.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@adriamics.com.
