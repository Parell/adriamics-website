Air-gapped mission design and flight simulation.

Frame is an open-source mission design and flight simulation project for exploring, testing, and refining engineering ideas. It brings design, simulation, optimization, verification, and operation into one workflow.

Features

Frame is intended to support the full engineering workflow:

  • Design missions around goals, constraints, and operating environments.
  • Simulate system behavior before committing to a physical design.
  • Optimize choices against performance goals and real-world constraints.
  • Verify designs against engineering requirements.
  • Operate systems in their intended environment.
