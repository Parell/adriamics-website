WHAT TO NOTICE

Fields reverse; energy moves outward

Arrowheads show the instantaneous direction of electric field (cyan), magnetic field (violet), and current (coral); each reverses after half a cycle. Amber markers show outward energy flow separately. Samples span multiple azimuths, and their size follows the dipole pattern: strongest broadside, zero along the antenna axis.

Thin-wire approximationIdeal resonant half-wave dipole in free space. Field strength is shaped by the dipole pattern, proportional to sin²θ.
