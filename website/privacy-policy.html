Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

This policy describes how the Adriamics website handles information when you use its study tools or contact us.

Information you send by email

If you email us, we receive the email address, name, and message you choose to include. We use it to read and respond to your inquiry. Email is handled by the mail services used by you and Adriamics.

Study preferences stored in your browser

The study pages use browser storage to remember recently visited subjects, completed practice problems, and your selected practice filter. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to Adriamics by the site. You can remove it using your browser's site data or storage controls.

Site hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. When a page is requested, Cloudflare may process technical request information under its own privacy practices. The site does not use accounts or a contact submission form.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Email inquiries are kept only as long as needed for review, follow-up, and related recordkeeping. The site has no server-side account or study-progress database.

Intellectual property

Unless a page or asset says otherwise, site content is licensed under the root CC0 1.0 Universal license. Third-party content remains subject to its own license or terms.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@adriamics.com.
